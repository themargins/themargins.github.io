---
title: Are You Righteous, Are You Kind
description: A reply to a question about goodness and suffering.
date: December 23, 2025
category: Poem
---

> "Are you righteous? Kind? Does your confidence lie in this? Are you loved by all? Know that I was, too. Do you imagine your suffering will be any less because you loved goodness and truth?"
>
> — James Jones

| I do not practice goodness as a wager with tomorrow,
| nor do I polish my virtues as coins to be spent against pain.
| If suffering comes, and it will, as it comes to all
| it will not ask what I believed, nor how carefully I lived.

| I choose goodness because it steadies my hands in this hour.
| I choose kindness because it teaches my breath how to remain.
| Truth is not a shield I raise against the future,
| but a lamp I carry so I may see where I am standing.

| If virtue should one day soften my suffering,
| let it be an accident, not an expectation.
| For what I seek is not exemption from grief,
| but a way to meet each moment without becoming smaller than it.

| And if pain must walk beside me,
| let it find me already whole,
| not because I was spared,
| but because I lived rightly while the road was still beneath my feet.

::: note
I wrote this in response to a good friend, a father to me, who kept sending me that quote at the oddest hours of the day.
:::
