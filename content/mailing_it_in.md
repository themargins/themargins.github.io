---
title: Autopilot
description: On the feeling of sleepwalking through life.
date: September 29, 2026
category: Poem
---

| I come to at the kitchen table
| with the kettle already boiled
| and no memory of filling it.

| The days arrive folded,
| the way laundry does,
| and I put them away
| without opening them.

| Someone says my name.
| I answer in the voice
| I keep for answering.

| Somewhere behind my eyes
| a light is on in a room
| I haven't entered in years.
| I can hear it humming.

| Tuesday and the Tuesday before
| share a window, share a sky.
| I move through both
| like a hand through water,
| leaving no shape.

| Then sometimes, without warning,
| a bird, a stranger's laugh,
| rain on hot pavement,
| and I am there all at once,
| startled, like a sleeper
| who has stepped onto a stair
| that wasn't there.

| Heart going.
| Every detail bright.
| The whole world saying,
| here, here, you're here.

| Then the fog folds back in,
| and I let it.
| I always let it.
